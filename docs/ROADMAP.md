# Moodify — Strategic Product Roadmap

## 1. Vision

Moodify evolves into an indispensable, quiet daily operating system for human taste, emotional rhythm, and personal logistics—delivering the warmth of a close, observant friend without surveillance, manipulation, or synthetic dependency.

## 3. Explicitly Deferred Capabilities (What We Do NOT Build Yet)

To maintain uncompromising product quality, trust, and safety, the following items are strictly out of scope for the current MVP:
- **Autonomous Unsupervised Purchasing:** All financial transactions require explicit human review.
- **Clinical Therapy Features:** Moodify will never position itself as medical care.
- **Unconstrained Social Feeds:** No public profiles, likes, follower counts, or algorithmic engagement traps.
- **3D Avatars & Synthetic Romance Mechanics:** Moodify is an honest tool, not a simulated human being.
- **Ad Networks & Sponsored Placements:** Pure subscription or value-aligned business model only.
